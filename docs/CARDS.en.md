## Keywords

| Keyword | Meaning | In a deck |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flying | Can't be blocked except by creatures with flying or reach. | yes |
| Reach | Can block creatures with flying. | yes |
| Haste | Can attack and {T} as soon as it comes under your control. | yes |
| Vigilance | Attacking doesn't cause it to tap. | yes |
| Trample | Combat damage beyond what's lethal to its blockers is dealt to the defending player. | yes |
| First strike | Deals combat damage before creatures without first strike. | yes |
| Double strike | Deals both first-strike and regular combat damage. | yes |
| Deathtouch | Any amount of damage it deals to a creature is enough to destroy it. | yes |
| Lifelink | Damage it deals also causes its controller to gain that much life. | yes |
| Menace | Can't be blocked except by two or more creatures. | yes |
| Defender | Can't attack. | yes |
| Flash | You may cast it any time you could cast an instant. | yes |
| Skulk | Can't be blocked by creatures with greater power. | yes |
| Undying | When it dies, if it had no +1/+1 counters on it, it returns to the battlefield with a +1/+1 counter on it. | yes |
| Hexproof | Can't be the target of spells or abilities your opponents control. | yes |
| Evolve | Gets a +1/+1 counter whenever another creature with greater power or toughness enters under your control. | yes |
| Prowess | Whenever you cast a noncreature spell, this creature gets +1/+1 until end of turn. | yes |
